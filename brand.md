# SAOS Brand: voice, design, and boundaries

## Brand identity in one sentence

**"Proven sovereignty on the chain. No promises. Receipts."**

SAOS speaks like a veteran trading engineer, not like a marketer. Every sentence carries a number, every number carries a source, every source can be verified independently. The silence of someone who does not need to promise.

## Brand voice (Tone of Voice)

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Precise, dry, factual | Excited, cartoonish |
| We show measurements and txids | We show price forecasts |
| We say "honestly awaiting funding" when that is the true state | We hide an imperfect status |
| "Measured", "verified", "proven" | "Revolutionary", "it changes everything", "to the moon" |
| The quiet confidence of infrastructure | Sales pressure, FOMO, countdowns |

### The three key words of the brand
1. **Sovereignty**, the system asks no chain for permission; it uses all of them as witnesses.
2. **Honesty**, every rejection is documented, every wait is declared, zero promises of returns.
3. **Proof**, a txid or it did not happen. Independent verification is the audience's right, not our favor.

## Language

- Hebrew as the base language; technical terms stay in English without forced translation: seal, stateRoot, anchor, relay, escrow, breaker.
- English for international material. Same voice, same discipline.
- **Zero emojis** in any professional material. Statuses appear as colored dots (green / yellow / red) or in words.

## Allowed and forbidden

**Allowed:**
- Quote measured numbers from the fact sheet, marked "measured"
- Attach a block explorer link to every claim
- Show an imperfect status ("TRON armed, awaiting funding"), it builds trust
- Use real system quotes (ALERT messages, haltReason) as proof of transparency

**Strictly forbidden:**
- A promise of returns, yield, APR, "passive", "income"
- A price forecast or a value comparison with other projects
- "Investment", "financial product", "advice". SAOS is technology infrastructure
- Using the Ethereum/Solana/Steem logos as if there were an official partnership. They are public verification layers, phrase it as: "anchors evidence on..."
- Emojis, exclamations of enthusiasm, multiple exclamation marks

## Uniform disclaimer (mandatory on every long page/post)

> SAOS is sovereign technology infrastructure in development. Nothing here is investment advice, an offer to buy a security, or a promise of any return. All data is measured or anchored on-chain and open to independent verification; theoretical data is explicitly marked as such. Use of public networks (Steem, Hive, Ethereum, Solana, TRON) as verification layers is not a partnership or an endorsement.

## The dash rule (owner directive, 2026-09-11)

Zero long separator dashes (em or en dash) in any sentence, paragraph, or number range. Alternatives: a comma, a colon, parentheses, a middle dot (·), a plain hyphen for ranges (30-44bps).
