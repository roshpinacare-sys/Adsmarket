# Ad pack v2: say less, prove more

> Replaces `ads-pack.md` (2026-09-12). The old pack carried honest bones but leaked internal figures:
> measured costs, an internal counter, a failure cause, operating cadence. Operator ruling: too exposed.
> The audit lives in `Defi/fleet/broadcasts/2026-09-28-agent-z-publishing-alignment.md`.
>
> Iron rules v2: zero emojis · zero promises of returns · zero internal figures (no costs, no balances,
> no counters, no cadences, no failure causes) · no em-dashes in published copy · no line a bot could
> have written · one claim per unit · a "come see" CTA, never a "buy" CTA · when a number is needed,
> it lives behind a link, inside a public receipt.

## G. Tone law (binding for every writer, human or agent)

1. Zero em-dashes in published copy. Periods, commas, full stops.
2. If a line sounds like a deck or a bot, cut it.
3. Short, long, short. No parallel triads. No "it's not just X, it's Y".
4. Say less than you know. Point at the receipt instead of unloading it.
5. Zero internal money. Zero cadences, counters, component counts, failure causes.
6. Hebrew reads like speech. If it reads translated, it gets cut.
7. One claim per unit. CTA is always "come see", never "buy".

---

## H. Visual directions v2

1. **Palette:** near-black background (#09090b), white text, gold accent (#d4af37). The feel of "an official document in the dark", not of a casino.
2. **Numbers:** monospace only, and only when the figure comes from a public receipt behind a link. No inline figures anywhere.
3. **Forbidden:** rockets, rising green graphs, people in sunglasses, "x100", countdowns, emojis, dashboards with balances, labeled server racks, price tags with exact figures, infrastructure diagrams of any kind.
4. **Encouraged:** journals, wax seals, paper receipts, scales, ledgers, a wall of paper, one blinking light in a dark room.
5. **Every ad = one claim.** Never mix notary + bridge + trading in the same unit.
